Selected Research
PROJECT 01

NNIPS Follow-Up — Prenatal Micronutrient Supplementation, Physical Activity, Sleep & Child Cognition

Follow-up study of children born to women enrolled in the Nepal Nutrition Intervention Project, Sarlahi (NNIPS) prenatal micronutrient supplementation trial, quantifying the long-term impact of supplementation on child cognition with objectively measured physical activity and sleep as candidate mediators. Aims span actigraphy-based characterization of activity and sleep in a rural LMIC setting, the effect of IFA, IFA+Zinc, and multiple-micronutrient regimens on those patterns, and whether they mediate cognitive outcomes. I appraised the protocols and codebooks, built the variable-selection framework, and prepared the dataset for analysis in R. Johns Hopkins Department of International Health.

25 protocols reviewed · 564 → 418 variables screened (26% reduction) · dataset prepared for analysis in R · documentation coordinated across faculty, doctoral students, and analysts
